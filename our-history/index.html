Four Generations.
One River at a time.

In 1915, “Grandpa” Glen Wooldridge built his first riverboat
to make the first-ever float down Oregon’s Rogue River.
It was the beginning of a lifetime of firsts.

His adventures stretched from Alaska’s Yukon to California’s Klamath, through Idaho’s powerful rivers and the seldom-traveled waters of British Columbia. At 79, Glen became the first to run the fearsome Hells Gate on BC’s Fraser River.

Now in its fourth generation, Wooldridge Boats carries that pioneering spirit forward. From our factory and showroom in Seattle, we build every boat in welded aluminum—built to order, built to last.

The story of Glen Wooldridge and Wooldridge Boats has been chronicled in books, magazines, and films for more than a century. Florence Arman’s The Rogue: A River to Run brings many of those adventures together with historical photographs and a foreword by Ted Trueblood, the celebrated Field & Stream associate editor who ran rivers and fished with Glen.

Ginger Rogers, Clark Gable, Herbert Hoover, Zane Grey, and countless others—famous and not—chose Glen Wooldridge for their fishing and adventure trips. At the root of it all was a Wooldridge boat.
