Agency & Work Boats

Built for the mission
PATROL · RESCUE · RESEARCH · SURVEY · UTILITY

Wooldridge has built patrol, rescue, research, survey, and utility vessels for government agencies and commercial operators for decades. Agency work is where our engineering shows: mission-specific layouts, heavy electrical packages, and hulls that hold up to daily professional use in unforgiving water.
