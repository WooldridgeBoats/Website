TELL US ABOUT YOUR BOAT — WE’LL PUT TOGETHER A LISTING FOR OUR USED BOATS PAGE

Thinking about selling? Tell us about your boat below and we'll put together a listing for our Used Boats page. We review every submission and follow up with next steps.

Takes about 8–10 minutes Progress saves on this device No account needed
We restored the draft you started earlier — pick up where you left off, or start fresh.

HOW THIS WORKS

  • Your ad connects you with buyers. Your contact info is posted right in the ad, so buyers reach you directly — Wooldridge doesn't manage the sale.
  • The ad is a flat $250 and has no expiration date. It runs until your boat sells.
  • A 1% service fee is due when your boat sells.
  • This form doesn't take payment. After you submit, we'll contact you to collect the $250 ad fee, and your ad goes live once payment is received.

Selling a motor, a trailer, or anything that isn't a boat? This form won't fit it — call (206) 722-8998 or email data@wooldridgeboats.com and we'll help you place that ad.
