Not For Practice

Since 1915, Wooldridge Boats has been guided by the belief that the best adventures begin where the easy water ends. Glen Wooldridge hand-built boats to explore the rivers few others would run—not for practice, but for the pursuit of what lay farther upstream.

Today, every Wooldridge is handmade, designed, and fabricated in the USA to carry that legacy into the places other boats can’t reach.

Over 110 Years of Innovation

From the Rogue River to Alaska’s Yukon, California’s Klamath, Idaho’s most formidable waterways, and the untouched rivers of British Columbia, Glen’s legacy is defined by “first-evers.” Today, based in Seattle, Washington, Wooldridge Boats continues that legacy—designing and building USA-made, high-performance aluminum and HDPE boats.
