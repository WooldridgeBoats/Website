== 1915
Glen Wooldridge builds his first river boat and makes the first-ever float down Oregon’s Rogue River. The company is born.
link: https://store.wooldridgeboats.com/products/the-rogue-a-river-to-run-book | Read the Whole Story: <i>The Rogue: A River to Run</i>

== 1920s
Glen takes a boat through the Coffee Pot in the early 1920s, photographed by Louise Stokes of the <i>Grants Pass Courier</i>.

== 1940s
Glen develops a method of stacking wood strips over a form, allowing better hull shapes without sacrificing strength. That boat is still on display at Rand Ranger Station.

== 1947
Running the Rogue: the first-ever trip up the Rogue River from Gold Beach to Grants Pass, with a 22-horsepower Evinrude.

== 1948
Glen and Ruell Hawkins make the first trip up Idaho’s Salmon River, “The River of No Return.”

== 1949
Glen starts a boat tour from the city park to Hell Gate: a 50-mile run in a 26-foot boat at $5.50 a person.

== 1950s
Glen Wooldridge Sr. develops the first Jackass lift for an outboard motor. The lift lets the boat run in shallower water.

== 1962
First outboard jet run up the Rogue River from Gold Beach to Grants Pass, Oregon.

== 1970s
Bob Wooldridge began bringing drift boats from Grants Pass to Seattle to sell, opening a new chapter for the family business. The boats were built by Bruce Wooldridge with Mike and Mark in Grants Pass.

== 1973
Bob Wooldridge opens the company’s first Seattle boat shop, bringing Wooldridge boatbuilding to Washington.

== 1975
At age 79, Grandpa Glen is the first to run up Hells Gate on BC’s Fraser River.
video: https://youtu.be/38WCO1ykWy0 | First Jet Boat to Run Up Hells Gate

== 1977
Glen Wooldridge develops the outboard jet tunnel.

== 1978
Glen Wooldridge and Dave Devore develop the first stainless steel outboard impeller.

== 1979
Four generations of boat builders working side by side.

== 2004
Wooldridge Boats moves to a new boat shop in South Seattle and continues to lead the way building custom aluminum boats.

== 2024
Another Wooldridge first: we created the <a href="/models/rogue/">Rogue</a>—the first production HDPE outboard jet boat—bringing more than a century of boatbuilding experience to a new material.

== 2026
The 2026 fleet: 18 production models from a 16′ tiller sled to a 26′ offshore boat, all still built in Seattle.
