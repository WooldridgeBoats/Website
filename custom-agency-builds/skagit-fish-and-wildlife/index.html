20′ Skagit Department of Fish and Wildlife

CUSTOM AGENCY BUILD
Gallery
Details

This build was commissioned by the Department of Fish and Wildlife for Electroshock fish monitoring

It is our 20′ Skagit outboard model with a 250 Yamaha outboard jet

This build had the following options / modifications

  • Custom console per agency’s request to fit electroshock equipment.
  • Bow rails for working forward

Check out our Skagit model page for more info on standard dimensions and specs
