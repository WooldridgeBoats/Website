Return Policy

RETURNS & REFUNDS

At Wooldridge Boats, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If you are unhappy with an item or believe there may be a problem with a product you received, please contact us directly so we can help.

Returns

New, unused products may be returned within 60 days of delivery with prior authorization from Wooldridge Boats.

Please contact us by phone or email before returning any item. Returns sent without prior authorization may not be accepted.

Returned products must be new, unused, and in their original condition.

Apparel Returns: Returned apparel must be new and unworn, with all original tags still attached.

Excluded Items

All items are eligible for return except “Special Order” items.

Digital Products

Online courses, digital downloads and subscriptions are one-time purchases and are not refundable.

Return Shipping

We do not charge a restocking fee on authorized returns. However, return shipping and postage costs are the responsibility of the customer unless otherwise agreed upon by Wooldridge Boats.

We recommend using a trackable shipping method and appropriately packaging the product to prevent damage during return shipment.

Refunds

Once an authorized return has been received and inspected, approved refunds will be issued to the original form of payment or by check, at the discretion of Wooldridge Boats. Please allow up to 10 business days for your refund to be processed once your return is received.

Original shipping charges and return shipping costs are not refundable unless otherwise agreed upon by Wooldridge Boats.

Problems or Defective Products

If, for any reason, you are unhappy with your purchase or believe a product is defective, please contact us before returning it. We would appreciate the opportunity to discuss the issue with you and determine the best way to resolve it.

When contacting us about a damaged, defective, or otherwise problematic product, please be prepared to provide photos of the product and the issue in question. This will help us evaluate the situation and assist you as quickly as possible.

We reserve the right to refuse any return if the item does not meet the above criteria, at the discretion of Wooldridge Boats.
