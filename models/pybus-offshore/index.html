Pybus Point Lodge

LODGE FLEET · ADMIRALTY ISLAND, ALASKA
Expedition-grade offshore hull, Alaska-proven.

Pybus Point Lodge fishes the remote saltwater around Admiralty Island in Southeast Alaska, and a fleet of Wooldridge boats takes its guests out every day. Lodge boats work harder than almost anything on the water: long days in open water, a steady rotation of anglers, and weather that can turn in an hour. Every boat we’ve built for Pybus is a welded-aluminum Wooldridge from our Seattle shop, set up for exactly that kind of work.

The Wooldridge Pybus fleet is ten boats strong: five 33′ Deepwaters, two 21′ Sport Offshores and three 20′ Sport center consoles. This page features those boats and the work they do for the lodge. Outfitting a lodge or charter operation of your own? Contact the factory to talk through your fleet.
