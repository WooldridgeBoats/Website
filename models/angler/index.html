Super Sport Offshore Angler

OFFSHORE · SSO HULL
Fish-first layout on the Super Sport Offshore hull.

The SSO Angler takes the proven Super Sport Offshore hull and dedicates the layout to fishing: open cockpit space, rod and gear storage where you need it, and a deck plan that keeps every corner workable when the bite is on.

Every Angler is configured and quoted with our team on the Super Sport Offshore platform. Ready to build one? Contact the factory to start a quote and talk through your layout.
